Salary Predictor

Predicting professional compensation through Linear Regression models. Analyze how variables like years of experience and specialized skills correlate with salary growth.

Project Overview

Data-Driven Salary Forecasting

This implementation demonstrates how Linear Regression identifies the linear relationship between independent features (experience) and a dependent continuous variable (salary).

Methodology: We utilize Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) to minimize the sum of squared residuals, ensuring the highest possible accuracy for our predictive line.

Feature Set

Years of experience, technical certifications, and role seniority.

Model Type

Simple & Multiple Linear Regression (Scikit-Learn implementation).

Objective

Reducing Mean Squared Error (MSE) to refine prediction reliability.
